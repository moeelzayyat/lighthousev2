## Description (under 1,000 characters)
Keep the light burning through the storm.

You and up to three friends keep a lonely lighthouse. Every night the sea sends crabs, skitters and stranger things onto the rocks: spitting eels, swelling puffer mines, ghost lanterns, a tide witch and her skitters. Sweep them back with the beam, focus it to burn the big ones, carry oil to the lamp, repair the tower and build cannons on the shore.

• Survive 7 nights to finish a voyage. Win once to unlock the 13-night Long Voyage.
• Every dawn you survive banks a Pearl that is yours to keep.
• Spend Pearls on harpoons, armour, beam colours and small boosts at the Quartermaster.
• Fill your logbook, take on a daily objective and climb the harbor board.
• Play solo or as a crew of four. Controller, touch and keyboard supported.

New to the lighthouse? Your first voyage starts right away, and the coach shows you what to do.
