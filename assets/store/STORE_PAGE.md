## Description
Keep the light burning through the storm.

You and up to three friends are the keepers of a lonely lighthouse. Every night the sea sends crabs, skitters and worse onto the rocks. Sweep them back with the beam, focus it to burn the big ones, carry oil to the lamp, repair the tower and build cannons on the shore.

• Survive 7 nights to finish a voyage. Win once to unlock the 13-night Long Voyage.
• Every dawn you survive banks a Pearl that is yours to keep, even if the storm wins later.
• Spend Pearls at the Quartermaster on new harpoons and armour.
• Vote on the storm forecast with your crew before you sail.
• Play solo or as a crew of four. Controller, touch and keyboard supported.

New to the lighthouse? Your first voyage starts right away, and the coach shows you what to do.
